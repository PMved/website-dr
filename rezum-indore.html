How It Works

The Science Behind Water Vapor Therapy

Rezum is an FDA-approved, minimally invasive treatment that uses convective water vapor to treat Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH).

1

Targeted Steam Delivery

A small device passes through the natural urethra. Targeted 9-second pulses of sterile water vapor are delivered directly into the obstructing prostate lobes.

2

Natural Cellular Shrinkage

The thermal energy safely disrupts cell membranes only within the targeted excess tissue without harming surrounding erectile nerves or the bladder sphincter.

3

Clear Urinary Flow Restored

Over the following weeks, your body naturally absorbs the treated tissue, opening the urethra wide so you can empty your bladder easily without straining or nighttime waking.

Clinical Self-Assessment

International Prostate Symptom Score (IPSS)

Answer these 7 quick questions to assess the severity of your urinary symptoms and see if you are an ideal candidate for Rezum.

1. Incomplete Emptying: How often have you had the sensation of not emptying your bladder completely?

2. Frequency: How often have you had to urinate again less than 2 hours after you finished urinating?

3. Intermittency: How often have you found you stopped and started again several times when you urinated?

4. Urgency: How often have you found it difficult to postpone urination?

5. Weak Stream: How often have you had a weak urinary stream?

6. Straining: How often have you had to push or strain to begin urination?

7. Nocturia: Over the past month, how many times did you typically get up at night to urinate?
