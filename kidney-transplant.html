The Kidney Transplant Journey at Rajshree Apollo Hospitals

1. Comprehensive Workup

Thorough immunological matching, HLA cross-matching, cardiac clearance, and legal documentation guidance under government transplantation protocols.

2. Laparoscopic Donor Surgery

The living donor undergoes a keyhole (laparoscopic) nephrectomy. This minimizes pain, ensures rapid recovery, and allows the donor to be discharged in just 2-3 days.

3. Post-Transplant Monitoring

Dedicated clean-room transplant ICU, precise immunosuppressive drug monitoring, and long-term surveillance to protect the new kidney for decades.
