The Right Treatment for Every Stone Size & Location

RIRS (Retrograde Intrarenal Surgery)

A high-definition flexible camera passes up through the bladder into the kidney. Laser fibers pulverize stones into dust.

Best for: Kidney stones up to 2 cm, upper ureter stones.

Mini-PCNL (Keyhole Surgery)

Through a tiny pencil-tip keyhole in the back, large or complex staghorn stones are broken and suctioned out cleanly.

Best for: Large stones (> 2 cm), staghorn calculus.

Laser Ureteroscopy (URS)

Quick endoscopic procedure to clear stones stuck in the ureter causing sudden acute colic pain. Day-care procedure.

Best for: Middle and lower ureteric stones.
