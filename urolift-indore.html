Mechanism of Action

How the UroLift® System Relieves Blockage

Unlike traditional prostate surgery which carves away obstructing tissue with heat or lasers, UroLift works like curtain ties to gently hold open the prostatic lobes.

1. Cystoscopic Access

Under light sedation or local anesthesia, a slender delivery device is introduced through the natural urinary channel (urethra). No external incisions or skin cuts are required.

2. Tiny Permanent Implants

The surgeon places micro-implants across the lateral lobes of the prostate. These implants pull the enlarged prostate tissue outwards, opening the obstructed urethra immediately.

3. Immediate Flow Restoration

The channel is instantly cleared. Because tissue is neither burned nor resected, bleeding is minimal, and most men return home within hours without a urinary catheter.

Clinical Candidacy

Who is Suitable for UroLift, and What are Its Limitations?

Dr. Chipde emphasizes evidence-based patient selection to guarantee optimal results and avoid unnecessary interventions.

Who May Benefit Most:

  • Men over 45 experiencing mild to moderate BPH urinary blockage.
  • Patients wanting to avoid or stop daily prostate medications (alpha-blockers/5-ARIs) due to dizziness or libido loss.
  • Men prioritizing the complete preservation of erectile and ejaculatory function.
  • Those seeking rapid return to work and routine physical activities (typically within 48 to 72 hours).

Clinical Limitations & Evaluation:

  • Not suitable for extremely massive prostates (>100 grams) where other modalities like Rezūm or HoLEP are better suited.
  • Requires cystoscopic evaluation to assess whether an obstructive middle lobe is present.
  • Active urinary tract infection or prostate cancer must be ruled out prior to procedure.
  • Permanent implants remain in the prostate; biocompatible and safe for MRI imaging.
