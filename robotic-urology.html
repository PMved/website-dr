Robotic Procedures Performed at Rajshree Apollo Indore

Robotic Radical Prostatectomy

For localized prostate cancer. 3D high-magnification robotic arms spare delicate neurovascular bundles, maximizing urinary continence and erectile recovery.

  • Nerve-sparing precision
  • Minimal blood loss

Robotic Partial Nephrectomy

Nephron-sparing surgery for renal tumors. Only the tumor is resected while saving the healthy remaining kidney, protecting long-term renal function.

  • Saves maximum healthy kidney
  • Short ischemia warm time
  • Quick return to active life

Robotic Pyeloplasty

Reconstruction of Pelviureteric Junction (PUJ) obstruction in adults and young patients. Precision microscopic suturing ensures high surgical success rates.

  • Fine tremor-free suturing
  • Negligible post-op pain
  • Fast recovery
