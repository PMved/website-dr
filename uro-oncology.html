Clinical Scope

Urological Cancers Treated

Early diagnosis, organ preservation whenever oncologically safe, and multidisciplinary coordination at Rajshree Apollo Hospitals Indore.

Prostate Cancer

Early screening with PSA and multiparametric MRI. Dr. Chipde pioneered the first robotic prostate surgery in MP, offering robotic nerve-sparing radical prostatectomy to maximize cure while safeguarding continence and erectile recovery.

Kidney Cancer (Renal Tumors)

Partial nephrectomy (nephron-sparing surgery) removes the tumor while preserving healthy functional kidney tissue. Radical nephrectomy and vena caval thrombus management for advanced lesions.

Bladder Cancer

Never ignore painless blood in urine. Early transurethral resection of bladder tumor (TURBT), intravesical immunotherapy (BCG), and radical cystectomy with orthotopic neobladder creation for muscle-invasive disease.

Testicular & Penile Cancers

High-cure rate protocols including high inguinal orchiectomy, retroperitoneal lymph node dissection (RPLND), and organ-sparing penile oncology in a compassionate, confidential environment.
