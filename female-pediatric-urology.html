Female Urological Health

Women's Urinary & Pelvic Health

Urinary leakage and recurrent infections are common but should never be accepted as a normal part of aging. Safe, effective medical and minimally invasive surgical solutions exist.

Stress Urinary Incontinence (SUI)

Urine leakage during coughing, laughing, exercising, or lifting. Evaluated with urodynamics and managed with pelvic floor rehabilitation or minimally invasive suburethral sling procedures (TOT / TVT).

Overactive Bladder (OAB) & Urge Leakage

Sudden, uncontrollable urinary urges and frequent daytime/nighttime visits. Managed with bladder retraining, advanced pharmacological therapies, and bladder Botox injections.

Recurrent Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs)

Investigating underlying anatomic causes, cystoceles, or post-menopausal changes to break the cycle of repeated antibiotic regimens with preventive protocols.

Childhood Urological Conditions

Pediatric Urology Services Offered

Delicate, high-precision surgical care for congenital and acquired urinary conditions in pediatric patients at Rajshree Apollo Hospitals Indore.

Undescended Testis (Cryptorchidism)

Early orchidopexy surgery (between 6 to 12 months) preserves future fertility and testicular health, performed via day-care or laparoscopic approaches.

PUJ Obstruction & Hydronephrosis

Pelvi-ureteric junction blockage causing swelling of the child's kidney. Managed with minimally invasive laparoscopic or open pyeloplasty to protect renal function.

Hypospadias & Phimosis

Reconstructive repair for abnormal urinary opening (hypospadias) and painless surgical circumcision (phimosis) with pediatric-friendly anesthesia and recovery protocols.
