Mechanism of Action

How the iTind™ Device Works

A gentle, non-surgical mechanical remodeling that reshapes the bladder neck and prostate without thermal burning or tissue destruction.

1. Day-Care Placement

Under local anesthesia or light sedation, the slender nitinol device is placed inside the prostatic urethra in an outpatient setting taking about 10 minutes.

2. Gentle Expansion (5–7 Days)

The device gently expands, exerting continuous pressure at 12, 5, and 7 o'clock positions. This creates three deep longitudinal grooves through natural tissue remodeling without burning.

3. Complete Removal

After 5 to 7 days, the device is gently collapsed and retrieved in the OPD clinic. The newly widened prostatic channel remains permanently open to allow free urine flow.

Clinical Evaluation

Who is Suitable for iTind & What Evaluation is Required?

Patient evaluation ensures you get the ideal procedure for your exact prostate volume and urinary anatomy.

Ideal Candidates for iTind:

  • Sexually active men with BPH who strongly wish to avoid retrograde ejaculation or erectile impairment.
  • Men who do not want any permanent foreign implants (such as clips or sutures) remaining inside their body.
  • Patients with mild to moderate prostate enlargement (prostate volume generally 25–75g).
  • Individuals seeking rapid recovery with minimal interruption to business or work.

Required Pre-Procedure Workup:

  • Uroflowmetry & PVR: Measuring peak urine flow rate and post-void residual urine volume.
  • Ultrasound KUB / Prostate: Precise volumetric calculation and bladder wall check.
  • Serum PSA Test: Screening to rule out concurrent prostate malignancy.
  • Cystoscopy: Assessing the presence of any obstructing median lobe.
